# 180DC initial screening rubric (DRAFT, to be agreed with the recruitment lead)

Score each criterion on the scale given in the instructions. Base every score only on
evidence in the CV and the application answers. If there is no evidence for a criterion,
give the lowest score and say "no evidence" rather than guessing.

Scale is 1–10. Descriptors anchor 9–10, 5–6 and 1–2; use 7–8 and 3–4 for in-between cases.

## Skills & experience (`skills_experience`)
Evidence: CV (work, internships, society roles, projects), answers.
- 9–10: Several relevant roles or projects (consulting, analysis, strategy, leadership) with concrete responsibilities and outcomes.
- 5–6: Some relevant experience, or experience that isn't relevant but is described well with transferable skills made clear.
- 1–2: Little or no experience listed, or only vague descriptions.

## Relevant academic ability (`academic_ability`)
Evidence: CV (degree, grades, modules, awards).
- 9–10: Strong grades (e.g. First/high 2:1 or equivalent) and/or awards, scholarships, or demanding relevant modules.
- 5–6: Solid grades, relevant subject, nothing standing out.
- 1–2: No academic information, or clear signs of weak performance.
Do not favour particular universities or subjects beyond what is relevant to consulting.

## CV & background (`cv_background`)
Evidence: the CV as a document.
- 9–10: Clear, well-structured, concise, achievement-focused (quantified results), no errors.
- 5–6: Readable and complete, but duties rather than achievements, or some formatting problems.
- 1–2: Disorganised, very sparse, or has many errors.

## Problem solving & critical thinking (`problem_solving`)
Evidence: mainly the application answers.
- 9–10: Structured answer that defines the problem, gives a concrete real example, explains the reasoning and trade-offs, and reflects on the result.
- 5–6: Relevant example, but the reasoning is only partly explained or the structure is loose.
- 1–2: Generic, off-topic, or doesn't answer the question.

## Social impact (`social_impact`)
Evidence: answers plus volunteering, charity, or community work on the CV.
- 9–10: Shows real understanding of social impact and a track record of acting on it (volunteering, NGO or social-enterprise work, initiatives they started).
- 5–6: Sincere interest with a little supporting evidence.
- 1–2: No evidence of interest beyond generic statements.

## Fairness rules
- Ignore name, gender, age, nationality, ethnicity, religion, disability, photo and any other protected characteristic.
- Don't penalise non-native English unless it makes the answer unclear.
- Don't give extra credit for length alone.
